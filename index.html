About Tradify

Tradify is a Final Year Project developed by students of the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) program, Session 2022–2026, at the Institute of Computer Science and Information Technology (ICS/IT), University of Agriculture, Peshawar.

The platform addresses a real gap in Pakistan's digital finance ecosystem — the lack of a unified, risk-free, locally-built trading education and community platform that combines live charts, portfolio simulation, and knowledge sharing.

Built using the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, Node.js) with TradingView chart integration, Tradify aims to democratize financial education for Pakistani youth.

  • Live market data for Gold, Bitcoin, Forex, and Global Stocks
  • Paper trading simulation with virtual PKR 100,000 balance
  • Community-driven trading analysis and blogging platform
  • TradingView-powered professional charts with multiple timeframes
  • Price alert system with in-app notifications
  • JWT-authenticated secure role-based access control
  • Designed for Pakistani context with local market focus
  • Open-source educational reference for BSIT/BSCS students
